# A branch keeps its colour where it rejoins the mainline

`1907253ef` · 2026-08-04

The side of a merge ran green from the merge dot down past its own
commits and then turned blue for the last stretch, where it curves back
into the commit both sides share.

The layout drew that arriving line in the colour of the commit it arrives
at rather than the colour of the line it comes from. The joining lane's
branch was there to be read — it was simply read after the lane had been
let go, and fell back to the merge's own.
