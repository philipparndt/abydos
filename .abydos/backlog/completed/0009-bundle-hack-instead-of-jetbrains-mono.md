# Bundle Hack instead of JetBrains Mono

`65da93c43` · 2026-07-30

Both licences permit redistribution, but shipping JetBrains' typeface inside an
alternative to their IDE is poor form regardless of what the licence allows.

Hack is a better fit on every axis: MIT (plus Bitstream Vera and public-domain
DejaVu components), so redistribution is unambiguous; a neutral source rather
than a vendor this competes with; drawn for source code; and no ligatures, which
is the safer default in a terminal.
