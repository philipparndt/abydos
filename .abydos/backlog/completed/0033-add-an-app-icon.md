# Add an app icon

`e47995926` · 2026-07-31

A prompt chevron and a caret: the two marks every editor and terminal
shares, in the window's own palette so the icon and the app agree. They
are drawn heavier than looks right at full size, because at 16 points
the whole icon is sixteen pixels across and thin strokes turn to grey
mush there.

Generated by Scripts/make-icon.py rather than checked in as artwork
alone. The icon is a handful of shapes, and a diff of the numbers that
describe them says more than a diff of a PNG — `make icon` redraws it.
The .icns is committed so a build needs no Python.
