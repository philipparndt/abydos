# The benchmark can be watched as well as read

`b72689eca` · 2026-08-04

`--fps` holds it to sixty frames a second, or to whatever number follows.

Nothing to do with measuring — the number the benchmark exists for is the
one you can only get by never waiting. But both of these are also nice to
look at, and at nine hundred frames a second neither of them looks like
anything: the fire is a flat glare and the rain falls faster than it can be
read. Held to sixty they turn back into the things they are pictures of.

The wait is measured from the start rather than added up frame by frame, so
a slow frame is caught up with instead of pushing every frame after it
later. The summary says which it was: "matrix held to 60" is not a result
to compare against anything.
