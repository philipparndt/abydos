# Settings pages have sections

`bb704c481` · 2026-08-04

A page is now a title and the sections under it, each with its own name
and its own card: Terminal is Appearance, Behaviour and tmux rather than
one list of nine things where the tmux switches happened to sit near each
other. A section's sentence goes under its name rather than inside the
card, where a note about the whole group read as a note about the first
setting in it.

Pages with no sections are unchanged — their rows still gather into one
card — so this is a shape sections can take rather than one every page
has to.
