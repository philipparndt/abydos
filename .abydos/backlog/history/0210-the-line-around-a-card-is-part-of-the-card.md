# The line around a card is part of the card

`2fa1ce8dc` · 2026-08-03

A layer keeps two colours, not one, and only the fill was being swapped — so
a settings card went light inside a dark outline, which is the one thing left
looking like the old theme. Borders swap too now, coloured views included:
they have both.

And the popovers over a maximised terminal are given the room they need. The
tree insets itself for a titlebar that a popover does not have, and left a
hand's width of nothing at the top; the panes that do not inset themselves
started hard against the edge instead. Neither now.
