# A palette of our own

`32af47ec7` · 2026-08-01

It began as IDEA's colours value for value, which is a fine place to
learn from and a poor place to stay: those values are that product's
signature. The shape of the scheme stays — quiet dark chrome, warm
keywords, green strings, blue for what is called — while the values are
ours: the greys carry more blue, and keyword, type and call are pulled
apart so they read as three colours rather than three shades.
