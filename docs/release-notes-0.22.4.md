# Abydos 0.22.4

## A song is packed with its samples

The song pane's Export menu has *Song and Samples as ZIP*: the song, the files
it includes and every sample it reads, in one zip beside it, with the paths in
its text pointing into the zip. What comes with installed software — Logic's
and GarageBand's instruments, Surge, plugins — is not packed; the pane says what
the song still needs, and the zip's `README.txt` lists it. It needs a `mat` that
packs: musik-as-text caacf43 or later.
