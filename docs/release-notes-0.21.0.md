# Abydos 0.21.0

## Sound files play, with their wave and spectrum

`.wav`, `.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.aac`, `.aif`, `.aiff`, `.flac` and `.caf` open as a
player showing the waveform per channel and a spectrogram, alone or together.
Click or drag to move the playhead, pinch or ⌥-scroll to zoom, and scroll to
move along; zoomed in, the wave and spectrum sharpen. The loop button repeats
without a gap. Formats macOS cannot decode, such as `.ogg`, keep the binary
notice.

## Playing, and moving around

Space plays and pauses, in the tab or on the file's row in the project tree —
for videos too, instead of opening Quick Look over a tab that already shows the
file. ← and → jump five seconds, one with ⇧. A sound keeps playing when you
switch tabs, with a speaker on its tab, and stops when its tab closes or another
sound or video starts.
